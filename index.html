The Short Version
Most organizations don’t need more marketing. They need the right fix. Sometimes it’s the offer.pipeline.message.pricing.partner. Step one is figuring out which.

That’s the part I enjoy most. Working out what’s actually going on, then pointing you at the best way to solve it.

About Me

I’ve been on both sides of the table.

I’ve spent 10+ years in sales and marketing, from running campaigns to knocking on doors, cold calling, building GTM strategies and working in CPG packaging. Most recently I’ve been building with AI, and there’s plenty more in between.

I’ve worked with scaling companies, associations, government bodies and established brands across Canada. That gives me a wide view of what works, what doesn’t, and who’s genuinely good at what.

Syed Co. is where I share that thinking. If you’ve got a sales or marketing challenge, I’m happy to talk it through. And if I’m not the right person to help, I’ll probably know who is.

Where I Can Help

Start with the problem.

You don’t need to know what service you’re shopping for. Tell me what’s going wrong and we’ll work backwards from there.

01

Revenue has stalled

Sales are flat or slipping and nobody’s quite sure why. We’ll look at the offer, the pipeline and the market to find where it’s leaking.

02

Marketing isn’t paying off

You’re spending, but you can’t see the return. Let’s figure out what’s worth keeping, what to cut, and what’s missing.

03

Sales feels messy

Deals go cold, or the process lives in a few people’s heads and won’t scale. Let’s build some structure around how you win customers.

04

Not sure who to hire

Agency, freelancer, in-house or fractional? I’ll give you my honest take and introduce you to people I trust.

05

Launching something new

New product, new market or a new brand. Talk it through with someone who’s been around a few launches.

06

Planning what’s next

Setting next year’s targets and not sure where to put the effort? A second opinion can save a lot of guesswork.
