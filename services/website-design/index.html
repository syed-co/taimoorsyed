Is this for you?

Who this is for, and who it isn’t.

A good fit if

  • Your site no longer matches the business you’ve become
  • You’re launching, rebranding or sending campaign traffic to a site that doesn’t convert
  • You want a site your team can actually update and improve

Probably not if

  • You want the lowest-cost template and nothing else
  • You’d like to skip strategy and jump straight to design
  • Nobody on your side can approve content and decisions
The problem

What this solves.

It looks dated or says the wrong thing

Visitors judge you in seconds. If the site doesn’t match the quality of the work, you lose people before they read a word.

People leave without doing anything

Traffic isn’t the problem if nobody calls, books or fills in the form. The page structure and message usually are.

It’s slow, hard to update or hard to find

A heavy site frustrates visitors and search engines alike, and a site only one person can edit stops improving.

Nobody owns it

Websites decay without an owner. A clear plan for who updates what keeps it useful long after launch.

Questions

Common questions.

How long does a website take?

Most projects land somewhere between 4 and 10 weeks, depending on size and how quickly feedback and content come in. Late content is the most common cause of delays, so we plan for it up front.

Do I need a new website, or can the current one be fixed?

Sometimes a few changes to structure, copy and speed do more than a rebuild. I’ll tell you honestly which one your site needs after a quick review.

Who writes the content?

It depends on the project. I can lead messaging and copy direction, you can supply it, or we split it. Good content matters more than good design, so it gets planned early.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes, that’s part of the plan. I choose the platform based on who’ll maintain the site, and I train your team before handover.

Who actually does the work?

I lead every project and stay close to the work. For specialist pieces like filming, development or illustration, I bring in people I trust, so you get the right skill for each part.

Do you work with organizations outside Vancouver?

Yes. I’m based in Vancouver, BC, and work with organizations across Canada, mostly remotely.
